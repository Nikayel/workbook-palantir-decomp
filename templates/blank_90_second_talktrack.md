# 90-Second Talk Track

**1. The Goal (15s)**
"The main problem we are solving is ________________________________."

**2. The MVP Approach (30s)**
"To solve this, my MVP focuses on ________________________________. I chose this because ________________________________."

**3. The Core Logic (30s)**
"The most critical part of the logic is ________________. I handled the edge case of ________________ by ________________."

**4. The Tradeoff (15s)**
"One tradeoff I made was ________________ instead of ________________. In the future, if scale increases, I would ________________."
