## What this is
This folder is a **playbook for the person who builds the workbooks** — not for the interns themselves. It tells a curriculum creator **how to create** the labs, **how many** to create, and gives **all the company-specific content** needed to make them authentic, plus **checklists** and **gold-standard samples** to clone.

It is the perfected, generalized successor to the existing Palantir SWE workbook in this repo (`/labs`, `/dsa_patterns`, …). Same soul — decomposition, Question/Assumption pairing, self-grading rubrics — but turned into a **repeatable system** across **3 roles × 10 companies**, grounded in instructional-design research and current (2024–2026) interview research.

> **Companies:** Google · Meta · Amazon · Microsoft · Apple · Palantir · Nvidia · Uber · Stripe · Atlassian
> **Roles:** Product Manager · Technical PM · Software Engineer (incl. "codebase-style" practical labs)

## The 60-second model
1. **Read** `02` (science) and `03` (standard) once.
2. **Pick** a `(company, role, tier)` and open its **company pack** + **role guide**.
3. **Clone** a **gold-standard sample** (or a `templates/` skeleton).
4. **Author** the 8 parts at the right fade tier; pull realism from the pack.
5. **Write feedback** (`solution_reasoning.md` + flashcards).
6. **Run the `08` checklists**, then publish.

## How much to build
~**100 labs** at full scope (~80 company-specific + ~20 shared), built in phases. Ship a ~**30-lab MVP** first — one high-signal track per company, signature-artifact-first. See `07`.

## The keystone rule
> A learner with no instructor must be able to **attempt every part, get specific feedback on every part, and know exactly what to do next.** If a lab can't, it isn't done.

## Research provenance
Built from five research streams (instructional-design science; PM, Technical-PM, and SWE intern interview processes; and per-company programs/culture/assessment), verified against 2024–2026 sources. Each company pack lists its sources and **confidence flags**; condensed research briefs live in the session scratchpad. Interview processes change — **re-verify each company pack every recruiting cycle.**
