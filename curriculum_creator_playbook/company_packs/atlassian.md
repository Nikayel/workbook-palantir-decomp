## 2. Culture & values (hiring signal)
Five values (verbatim): **"Open company, no bullshit" · "Build with heart and balance" · "Don't #@!% the customer" · "Play, as a team" · "Be the change you seek."** Plus **Team Anywhere** (permanent distributed work; Fortune #1 Best Co 2025). Values are explicitly used in hiring.

## 3. What's distinctive
- **The Values interview is a real, scored, standalone GATE** — often run by someone **outside your target team**; technical strength **cannot override a "No"** ("no brilliant jerks"). This is the most Atlassian-specific artifact.
- **Craft over flawless syntax** (SWE): reasoning, tradeoffs (verbosity vs optimization), communication, and **voluntarily writing test cases** are rewarded; "missing a line isn't a deal-breaker."

## 4. Assessment artifacts to replicate
- **OA:** HackerRank (2–4 Q, Easy–Medium, 60–90 min) **and/or** a **"World of Atlassian" SJT** (situational judgement probing values) + numerical/verbal reasoning.
- **Karat** third-party technical phone screen (DSA + clean implementation) — a mandatory gate.
- **Loop (~3–4):** a **"Craft" coding** interview · **Code design / LLD** (Snake game, cab-booking, notification systems) for senior/grad · **Values interview** · HM/leadership.
- **PM/TPM:** product case + **~48-h execution take-home** (prompt states exactly what's assessed) + leadership + values.

## 5. Role tracks
**SWE.** Algorithmic + **craft/LLD + write-your-own-tests**. Over-index: practical OOD/LLD, trees/graphs, arrays/strings/hashmaps, occasional concurrency. Reward voluntarily-written tests and clean, maintainable code. Languages: any.
**PM / APM.** Tie every decision to **customer impact + a value**; PM craft framed as **General Manager / Artist / Scientist**; bring the case answer "full circle" to a value. KPIs, product-health metrics, **experimentation/A-B**, SQL-adjacent reasoning.
**Technical PM.** Heavier execution/craft + the **48-h take-home** (disciplined scoping + explicit tradeoffs + crisp writing a technical audience can act on).

## 6. Lab build list
- SWE *workbooks*: `01` craft coding + **write-your-own-tests** lab (Tier 1) · `02` **LLD** (notification system / cab-booking) (Tier 2) · `03` **Values interview** lab (STAR mapped to all 5 values) (Tier 1, story-bank template) · `04` "World of Atlassian" SJT prep (Tier 2) · `05` **timed mock-OA** (Tier 3). Craft + LLD are system-building; pure DSA pattern prep → shared **drill kit**.
- PM/APM: `01` experimentation/A-B + KPI lab (Tier 2) · `02` values-mapped product case (Tier 2) · `03` Values panel (shared).
- Technical PM: `01` 48-h execution take-home (Tier 3) · `02` KPI/SQL reasoning (Tier 2).

## 7. Authenticity notes
Real surfaces: Jira, Confluence, Bitbucket, Trello, Loom, Rovo (AI). B2B/SaaS framing. **Rehearse STAR mapped to all 5 values** — especially a **mistake you owned** (Open company, no bullshit), **advocating for the customer when inconvenient** (Don't #@!% the customer), **team over personal credit** (Play, as a team). Reward voluntarily-written tests in SWE keys. **2025–26 context:** ~1,600 layoffs (Mar 2026, ~10%, R&D-heavy) **vs** a stated pledge to hire more new grads — volatile; verify req volume.
