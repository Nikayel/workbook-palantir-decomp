## 2. Culture & values (hiring signal)
**The 16 Leadership Principles (LPs)** are the backbone of *every* interview; behavioral answers are scored against them. Full list: Customer Obsession · Ownership · Invent and Simplify · Are Right, A Lot · Learn and Be Curious · Hire and Develop the Best · Insist on the Highest Standards · Think Big · Bias for Action · Frugality · Earn Trust · Dive Deep · Have Backbone, Disagree and Commit · Deliver Results · **Strive to Be Earth's Best Employer** · **Success and Scale Bring Broad Responsibility** (last two added 2021). **Most interview-load-bearing:** Customer Obsession, Ownership, Dive Deep, Invent and Simplify, Bias for Action, Are Right A Lot, Earn Trust, Have Backbone, Deliver Results.

Two more defining mechanisms: the **Bar Raiser** (a trained, neutral interviewer from outside the hiring team with **veto power**) and **Working Backwards** (write the customer-facing outcome first).

## 3. What's distinctive
- **LP-mapped STAR is the single biggest differentiator across ALL roles — even SWE.** ~25% of candidates who clear the technical bar are still rejected on behavioral. The whole curriculum should drill a deep, flexible STAR bank.
- **Writing culture.** PowerPoint is effectively banned; ideas are vetted via **6-page narratives** and the **PR-FAQ** (a mock press release < 1 page + an FAQ ≤ 5 pages, 10+ drafts).

## 4. Assessment artifacts to replicate
- **OA = highest-elimination stage:** Coding (2 problems, ~70–90 min, correctness + efficiency, compilable, copy-paste monitored) **+ Work Simulation (~50 min)** — a simulated inbox triaging emails/debugging, **scored against the LPs** — **+ Work Style Assessment** (values survey). Strong coders who bomb the simulation get rejected.
- **PR-FAQ** (PM) and **6-pager** narrative — the signature PM artifact.
- **STAR bank** of 10–12 flexible, individual-contributor (use "I"), quantified stories — not one-per-LP.

## 5. Role tracks
**SWE.** Algorithmic — heavy on **sliding window, graph/tree traversal (BFS/DFS), strings, greedy/heaps, hash maps**. But author the **Work-Simulation + LP behavioral** as first-class labs; SWE interns are heavily judged on LPs.
**PM.** LP-heavy behavioral + product cases + **PR-FAQ writing**. Most-weighted LPs: Customer Obsession, Bias for Action, Dive Deep.
**Technical PM (PMT).** ~½ behavioral (LPs), ½ technical — but technical = **architecture/system-design *explainers*, NOT coding** ("how do the internet/APIs work," CS principles, simplify complex tech). MBA interns: often two back-to-back 45-min sessions.

## 6. Lab build list
- SWE *workbooks*: `01` **Work-Simulation inbox-triage LP lab** (Tier 2) · `02` **design-a-DS/LLD** lab (Tier 2) · `03` **LP-STAR behavioral** (Tier 1, story-bank template) · `04` **timed mock-OA** (Tier 3). Sliding-window/graph/string pattern prep → shared **DSA drill kit**, *not* workbooks.
- PM: `01` **PR-FAQ** lab (Tier 1 worked → Tier 2) · `02` product case + Dive-Deep metrics (Tier 2) · `03` LP-STAR (shared with SWE).
- Technical PM (PMT): `01` **architecture explainer** ("design a URL shortener / rate limiter" conceptually, no code) (Tier 2) · `02` PMT phone-screen mock (½ LP, ½ technical) (Tier 3).

## 7. Authenticity notes
Real surfaces: Amazon retail, Prime, AWS, Alexa, Kindle, logistics/FBA. **Always anchor to a customer** (Customer Obsession). Teach PR-FAQ exactly: press release first, FAQ second, customer experience before tech. Model STAR with quantified results and "I." **2025–26 context:** big corporate cuts but ~11k devs/eng/interns planned for 2026 → higher "why you" scrutiny; tightened anti-AI OA proctoring; 5-day RTO (Jan 2025).
