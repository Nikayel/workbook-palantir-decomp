# Company Pack — Apple

## 1. Snapshot
| Program | For | Length / timeline |
|---|---|---|
| **Team-specific internships** (SWE, hardware, design, ML…) | undergrad/grad **returning to school** after (or internship = last grad requirement) | ~12+ wk; team-dependent; some flexible/longer |
> **Not a monolithic rotation** — you embed on ONE team. The **WWDC Swift Student Challenge is a coding *competition*, NOT an internship** (a credential/portfolio signal only).

## 2. Culture & values (hiring signal)
**Secrecy/confidentiality** (need-to-know; you often won't know your exact team/project until late) · **craft / quality / attention to detail** · **functional organization** (report to a function, not a business unit; deep specialists, few generalist PMs) · the **DRI (Directly Responsible Individual)** — one named owner per decision. Stated values: accessibility, privacy, environment, inclusion, education.

## 3. What's distinctive
- **Team/domain depth > generic LeetCode grind.** Loops include domain-specific/practical rounds mapped to what that team does daily.
- **Apple has few classic PMs** — the analog is the **EPM (Engineering Program/Project Manager ≈ TPM)** + **PMM (Product Marketing ≈ classic "what to build")**. Figure out which you're prepping. For SWE, signal **domain alignment + DRI ownership + craft**.

## 4. Assessment artifacts to replicate
- **No standardized company-wide OA.** Each team runs its own loop; some give a take-home (HM discretion). When an OA exists it's HackerRank/CodeSignal-ish (~2 medium, ~90 min) — but many 2025 intern reports have **no OA at all**.
- Loop (interns): recruiter (~15–30 min: "Why Apple? favorite product?") → 1–2 technical phone → **lighter 2–4-round loop** (manager/behavioral + 1–3 technical). **Variance is the rule.**
- **Resume/project deep dive** = a hallmark round — interviewers drill something off your resume, sometimes more than the coding.

## 5. Role tracks
**SWE.** LeetCode-pattern leaning **practical**, mostly Medium, with **data-structure implementation** (merge sort + complexity; linked-list ops). Team-specific over-index: **iOS → memory mgmt (ARC, retain cycles, weak/unowned), optionals, closures, protocols**; **embedded/silicon → pointers & arithmetic, stack vs heap, bit manip, `volatile`/`const`, "implement malloc."** Languages team-specific: **Swift/Obj-C** (iOS), **C/C++** (embedded, sometimes IDE-less).
**PM/PMM.** Teardown + improve an iconic Apple product (Watch/TV/Pay/Music); design taste; reason about tradeoffs the real team faced. Process sparse — proxy from FT.
**Technical PM / EPM.** Program/execution + behavioral + a **technical presentation** + a panel that probes **system architecture on your own projects**. More TPgM than classic PM-T.

## 6. Lab build list
- SWE: `01` practical DSA + implement-a-data-structure (Tier 1) · `02` **iOS-skin lab** (ARC/optionals/closures) (Tier 2) · `03` **embedded-skin lab** (pointers/bit-manip/"implement malloc") (Tier 2) · `04` resume-deep-dive + behavioral prep (Tier 2).
- PM: `01` teardown/improve an Apple product (Tier 1) · `02` product-tradeoffs case (Tier 2).
- Technical PM / EPM: `01` program-execution + dependency/risk lab (Tier 2) · `02` technical-presentation prep (Tier 2).

## 7. Authenticity notes
Real surfaces: iPhone/iOS, Watch, Vision Pro, Apple Pay, Music, Maps, Silicon (M-series). Pick the **team-authentic language** (Swift for iOS, C/C++ for systems). Model **DRI ownership** in behavioral keys; respect the secrecy culture (candidate thrives with limited context). **Honest flag: intern data is genuinely sparse/team-dependent — present round counts as a 2–4 range.**

## 8. Sources & confidence
PM/TPM/SWE + company briefs; interviewing.io, igotanoffer, Exponent, Glassdoor/Taro 2025 intern reports, dataford embedded. **Confidence:** medium overall; **low** on a fixed loop (team-dependent by design) — emphasize variance to learners.
