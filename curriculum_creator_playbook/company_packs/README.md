# Company Packs

> **Audience:** the curriculum creator.
> **Purpose:** one pack per target company = the **raw material to make practice authentic** for that company across PM, Technical PM, and SWE intern tracks. A pack is *not* a workbook; it's the company-specific facts, values, assessment formats, and lab-build list you combine with a role guide (`04`/`05`/`06`) and the authoring standard (`03`) to write workbooks.

## The 10 companies
Google · Meta · Amazon · Microsoft · Apple · Palantir · Nvidia · Uber · Stripe · Atlassian

## Pack template (every pack follows this)
1. **Snapshot** — intern programs, eligibility, timeline (table).
2. **Culture & values** — the stated hiring signal.
3. **What's distinctive** — the 1–2 things that most differentiate a strong candidate.
4. **Assessment artifacts** — the named formats to replicate in labs.
5. **Role track: SWE / PM / Technical PM** — what each tests *here* + lab notes.
6. **Lab build list** — exactly what to author, with tiers (feeds `07`).
7. **Authenticity notes** — languages, real product surfaces, dos & don'ts.
8. **Sources & confidence** — and what's a proxy/unverified.

## How to read the confidence flags
Interview processes change and many official pages block scraping. Each pack flags where intern-specific data is **sparse** (often substituted with the full-time loop). **Always re-verify dates, OA cutoffs, and AI-in-interview policy on the live careers page before a cohort.** Treat percentages (conversion, acceptance) as directional.
