# Company Pack — Meta

## 1. Snapshot
| Program | For | Length / timeline |
|---|---|---|
| **SWE Intern** | undergrad/grad (targets ~Dec 2026–Jul 2027 grads) | ~12 wk (May–Aug); **rolling — apply early** |
| **Meta University (MU)** | freshmen/sophomores from underrepresented groups, ≥1 programming course, US work auth | ~8 wk, Menlo Park |
| **PM Intern / RPM** (Rotational Product Manager) | early-career, diverse academic backgrounds | RPM = 18 mo (3 rotations) + bootcamp; ~97% don't get an interview |

## 2. Culture & values (hiring signal)
Six core values (Zuckerberg, Feb 2022): **Move Fast** (now "move fast *together*") · **Focus on Long-Term Impact** · **Build Awesome Things** · **Live in the Future** · **Be Direct and Respect Your Colleagues** · **Meta, Metamates, Me** (company + teammates over self). Employees are "Metamates." Current signals: aggressive **AI** investment, in-person focus, candid/direct feedback.

## 3. What's distinctive
- **Speed + fluency** on medium/hard coding (the pool skews to Blind-75 / "Top 75"); interns are expected to land **2 problems fast** per round.
- **Execution is data-driven, not project management.** PM/RPM rounds are unusually **quantitative** (metric selection, A/B). Build that into PM labs.

## 4. Assessment artifacts to replicate
- **CodeSignal multi-stage OA** (~90 min) — often one complex problem in **4 progressive stages** (e.g., build an in-memory DB / file-storage service). *This is itself a codebase-style spine — use the ICF model (`06` §3) even for Meta's "algorithmic" track.*
- **CoderPad** phone screen (~45 min, 2 medium problems, ~35 min coding).
- **"Jedi" behavioral round** — structured 45 min scored on: resolving conflict, growing continuously, embracing ambiguity, driving results, communicating effectively.
- **AI-Enabled Coding Interview (Oct 2025):** replaces **one** of the two onsite coding rounds — ~60 min, three-panel CoderPad (files / editor / AI assistant), approved models include GPT-5, Claude Sonnet 4/4.5, Gemini 2.5 Pro, Llama 4. You must **drive the solution and catch the AI's mistakes**; pure prompting fails. *(Began invite-only at E4–E5; intern applicability unconfirmed — flag.)*

## 5. Role tracks
**SWE.** Algorithmic, **speed-weighted**. Over-index: arrays/strings, hash maps, two-pointers/sliding window, trees/graphs (BFS/DFS), heaps, intervals, binary search. No system design for interns. Author at least one **AI-enabled** lab (use AI, but the rubric rewards driving + catching errors).
**PM / RPM.** Two pillars: **Product Sense** (deep "what would you build for X" with rich follow-ups) and **Execution** (data-driven tradeoffs, metric selection/goal-setting, A/B). Plus **Leadership & Drive** behavioral. Add an **AI-prototype** design lab.
**Technical PM.** No separate TPM track; strengthen the PM loop with technical-feasibility depth (metrics, light API/data).

## 6. Lab build list
- SWE *workbooks*: `01` **CodeSignal 4-stage in-memory-DB** (codebase / ICF spine) (Tier 2) · `02` AI-enabled coding lab (Tier 2) · `03` Jedi behavioral (Tier 2) · `04` **timed mock-OA** (Tier 3). The two-medium *speed* prep → shared **DSA drill kit**, *not* a workbook.
- PM/RPM: `01` "Build for billions" product sense (Tier 1) · `02` execution/metrics + A/B (Tier 2) · `03` design+prototype an AI feature (Tier 2) · `04` Leadership & Drive STAR (Tier 2).

## 7. Authenticity notes
Real surfaces: Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp, Threads, Reels, Quest/AR, Llama/Meta AI. Frame product prompts at **billions-of-users** scale. For Execution, force a **metric definition + guardrail + A/B design**, not vibes. Model "Be Direct" candor in behavioral keys.
