Treat the name as in-flux and confirm on the live listing. No formal **Technical-PM intern** title — route technical-PM-leaning candidates through APM or SWE.

## 2. Culture & values (hiring signal)
Four scored attributes: **General Cognitive Ability (GCA)** — your *process* on ambiguous problems (weighted highest); **Role-Related Knowledge (RRK)** — domain/technical depth (weighted lowest); **Leadership** — specifically *emergent* leadership; **Googleyness**. On **Dec 18 2024**, Pichai said "Googleyness" had grown too broad and reframed it to six points: *Mission First; Make Helpful Things; Be Bold & Responsible; Stay Scrappy; Hustle & Have Fun; Team Google.* The older Bock signals still drive the read: intellectual humility, comfort with ambiguity, bias to action, conscientiousness, collaboration, user-first.

## 3. What's distinctive
- **Reasoning aloud > landing the optimal answer.** The bar is famously a **~3.5/4 average** across structured 1–4 rubrics, and an **independent Hiring Committee** votes on a packet — so *how* you scope and narrate matters more than the final solution.
- **Googleyness can veto.** A "No Hire" on it can sink a strong technical loop. Bake authentic Googleyness signals into behavioral labs.

## 4. Assessment artifacts to replicate
- SWE intern: coding **in a plain Google Doc** (no autocomplete/highlighting) — author a lab that forces clean code without IDE aids. 2×45-min screens + team match, **no system design**.
- Structured rubric + Hiring-Committee framing (decouple "interview" from "decision" in your reflection prompts).
- *(Emerging, weakly sourced:)* AI-assisted coding rounds reported for 2026 — flag as uncertain.

## 5. Role tracks
**SWE.** Algorithmic, easy→medium/hard. Over-index: arrays/strings, hash maps, trees/graphs (BFS/DFS), recursion, two-pointers/sliding window, DP. Emphasis on **GCA narration + ambiguity handling**. Language: learner's choice.
**PM / APM.** Product Sense (a "what would you build for X"), Analytical/Execution (metrics, estimation, prioritization), structured communication, **Googleyness**. Light/no system design for interns.
**Technical PM.** Folded into the PM loop + GCA — reason about technical tradeoffs and feasibility, **don't code**. System-design-lite weighted on **estimation/scoping**.

## 6. Lab build list
- SWE *workbooks*: `01` **design-a-DS** (LRU) under the plain-doc / narrate-aloud constraint (Tier 1) · `02` ambiguity-narration **decomposition-lite** lab (Tier 2) · `03` **timed mock-OA** (Tier 3). Pure DSA → the shared **drill kit** (pattern map + LeetCode/NeetCode + flashcards), *not* workbooks.
- PM: `01` product-sense "Design X for Google users" (Tier 1) · `02` metrics/NSM (Tier 2) · `03` estimation (Tier 2) · `04` Googleyness behavioral (Tier 2).
- Technical PM: `01` system-design-lite with heavy scoping/estimation (Tier 2) · `02` technical-tradeoff explainer (Tier 2).

## 7. Authenticity notes
Real surfaces: Search, Maps, YouTube, Photos, Workspace, Android. For Googleyness, model "intellectual humility + user-first + collaboration"; **don't** model name-dropping or arrogance. Plain-doc coding is a genuine differentiator — replicate it.
