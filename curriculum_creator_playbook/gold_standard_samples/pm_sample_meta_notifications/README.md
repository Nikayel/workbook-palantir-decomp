# Rank the Notifications Feed (product sense + execution)

**Company / role / track:** Meta · Product Manager (PM / RPM)
**Simulates:** Meta's two PM pillars — **Product Sense** ("what would you build for X") and **data-driven Execution** (metrics, tradeoffs, A/B).
**Tier:** 1 (worked) · **Difficulty:** medium

Target time: 70 minutes · Minimum: 45 · Deep version: 100

> **Why this is the PM gold-standard sample.** Meta interviews are unusually **quantitative** — execution is "data-driven, not project management" — and product prompts are framed at **billions-of-users** scale. This lab makes the learner do both: turn an ambiguous space into a **prioritized** solution (product sense) **and** defend it with a **North Star + guardrail metric and an A/B test** (execution). The deliverable is a **brief + metric tree, never code** — that's the PM line.

## What you'll practice
User segmentation, naming real pains before solutioning, RICE prioritization (with an explicit cut), choosing a North Star + guardrail metric, and designing an A/B test that could actually ship.

## Time plan
Part 0–1 forethought + clarifying (10) · Part 2 decomposition/segmentation (12) · **Part 3 product design + metric (23)** · **Part 4 write the brief + metric tree + A/B (15)** · Part 5 reasoning (5) · Part 6 simulation (10) · Part 7 self-grade (5).

## How to use
1. Open `workbook.md`; fill every `[blank]`. Don't open the solution.
2. Produce the artifact in `artifacts/metric_tree_and_brief.md`.
3. Practice the Part 6 curveballs **out loud** (Meta scores communication + "Be Direct").
4. Only then read `solution_reasoning.md`.
5. Self-grade on the **PM rubric**. Review `flashcards.md` today, then day 3/7/14.
