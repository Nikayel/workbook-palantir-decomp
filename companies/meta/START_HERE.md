## What Makes Meta Distinctive

### For SWE

**SPEED is the differentiator.** Meta phone screens give you 45 minutes for 2 medium LeetCode problems. The expectation is not "get to an answer" — it's "finish both, walk me through complexity, and handle a follow-up." If you can only do 1 problem in 45 minutes, you are not at Meta pace yet.

Practice target: 2 mediums in 35 minutes, leaving 10 minutes to explain and answer follow-ups.

**CodeSignal 4-Stage ICF (Industry Coding Framework):** Meta's OA is not a typical LeetCode dump. The ICF format presents 4 progressive levels of a single problem — each level builds on the prior. L1–L2 are typically straightforward; L3–L4 add complexity (history, range queries, concurrency hints). Strategy: finish L1 and L2 completely before touching L3. Partial L3 > no L4.

**"Jedi" Behavioral Round:** One full round (30–45 min) dedicated to behavioral questions mapped to Meta's values. Have 5–8 STAR stories ready. Stories should demonstrate: speed under uncertainty, cross-functional collaboration, shipping something that failed (and what you learned), and a time you pushed back.

**AI-Enabled Coding Interview (Oct 2025+):** Meta replaced one round with an AI-assisted coding session. You may use AI tools during the problem, but the interviewer evaluates whether you understand and can explain every line. "Copilot-and-pray" fails here — the follow-up questions are designed to expose if you didn't understand what the AI wrote.

### For PM

**Data-driven, not project management.** Meta PM interviews are product sense + execution + leadership. They expect you to define success metrics before features, not after. "I would launch this feature" is incomplete — "I would launch this feature if DAU/MAU improves by X% with p-value < 0.05" is the bar.

**A/B testing fluency is required.** You must understand: control vs. treatment, novelty effects, network effects on A/B validity, and how to read a metric movement without overclaiming.

## Before You Start

**Calibrate your speed first.** Open LeetCode. Set a 35-minute timer. Pick 2 medium problems at random. Go. Do not read hints. If you finish both with time to spare: you're ready for Labs 02+. If you finish one and stall: start with Lab 01 and do it twice a day until your pace is there.

**Practice Jedi behavioral stories.** Write 6 stories in STAR format (Situation, Task, Action, Result). Each story should be usable for multiple Meta values. Story length target: 2 minutes spoken aloud. Test yourself: record a voice memo and play it back.

**Know Meta's values verbatim.** You will be asked "tell me about a time you moved fast" — you must know that "Move Fast Together" means *with alignment*, not *solo recklessness*. The nuance matters in a Jedi round.

**For PM:** Build your metric intuition now. Before every app you use, ask: "What is the north star metric? What would a 1% drop in DAU mean for this team?" Make this a daily habit.
