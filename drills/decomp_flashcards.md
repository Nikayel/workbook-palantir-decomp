# Decomposition Flashcards

**Q: How do you handle an ambiguous prompt?**
A: Clarify the Goal, Users, and the biggest Bottleneck.

**Q: What if the interviewer won't answer your clarifying question?**
A: State an assumption clearly and move forward. "Assuming the data fits in memory..."

**Q: How do you prioritize features?**
A: Focus on what solves the immediate bottleneck for the primary user. Everything else is V2.
